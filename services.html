Our services

Five engagements. One goal: growth that holds.

Every engagement starts with an audit of your business, then a plan built specifically for where you are — with hands-on support to build it.

01 — Strategy

Growth strategy

A clear, prioritized roadmap for the next 12 months — built around your team's actual capacity, not a generic playbook. We identify the two or three moves that will actually move the business, and cut the rest.

  • Full business and market audit
  • 90-day and 12-month growth roadmap
  • Prioritized initiative list with owners and metrics
  • Quarterly strategy reviews
02 — Operations

Operating systems

We install the metrics, cadences, and accountability structures that let you scale without everything breaking — dashboards, meeting rhythms, and decision-making processes that run without you in the room.

  • KPI dashboard and reporting cadence
  • Team meeting and review structure
  • Delegation and decision-rights mapping
  • Hands-on implementation with your team
03 — Coaching

Executive coaching

Weekly 1:1 sessions to sharpen decision-making, delegation, and the mindset shifts that come with each new stage of growth — for founders who are the biggest lever and the biggest bottleneck in the business.

  • Weekly 1:1 coaching sessions
  • Leadership and delegation development
  • Direct access between sessions
  • Optional leadership-team workshops
04 — Expansion

Franchise & expansion systems

The documentation and systems a brand needs to replicate itself reliably — whether opening a second location in Egypt or entering a new market abroad.

  • Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
  • Training and operations manuals
  • Franchise kits for new locations and partners
  • Support for scaling inside and outside Egypt
05 — Training

Team training & supply chain

Hands-on courses and workshops for your team, with a focus on supply chain — the operational backbone that keeps growth from breaking down under pressure.

  • Supply chain fundamentals and best practices
  • Team workshops and skills training
  • Process documentation for key roles
  • Ongoing coaching as the team grows
